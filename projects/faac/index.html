2026 — Web Design

FAAC

The Fine Arts Advisory Council website supports arts education across all six Mercer Island schools, serving local parents, teachers, donors, and community members. The redesign replaces a cluttered, unresponsive layout with streamlined navigation and clear calls to action, making it effortless for users to donate, buy tickets, and volunteer.

01. Problem Identification

From Clutter to Professional Legitimacy

The Challenge: The original site (mifineartsed.org) suffered from cluttered layouts, poor mobile responsiveness, and complex navigation.

The Friction: Weak visual hierarchy made it difficult for parents and teachers to find essential information, such as ticket sales, donation links, the contact form, and volunteer opportunities.

Brand Misalignment: The site lacked a cohesive visual identity that reflected the “creative energy” of the Mercer Island arts community.

02. Strategic Goal

Building Trust & Driving Community Action

Core Objectives: Guided by the goals to “Inform, Build Trust, and Guide to Action,” the FAAC redesign focused on intuitive navigation and clear calls to action (CTAs) to boost engagement.

Legitimacy through Transparency: To build donor confidence, this section establishes clear accountability by showing how every contribution directly impacts students. It outlines how funds are actively distributed to foster creativity, sustain essential arts curricula, and support all six Mercer Island schools.

03. Solution Design

A Modern, Restructured Platform

Design Accelerator: From discovery through launch, the project followed an iterative UX process—defining goals, wireframing in Figma, and implementing in Wix with a focus on accessibility and mobile responsiveness.

Information Hierarchy: Restructured the content to guide users through a logical flow: from Awareness (mission and arts impact) to Action (buying tickets, donating via GiveButter, or signing up to volunteer).

Color Palette

Reflecting Creative Energy and Enhancing Accessibility

Visual Identity: A vibrant, high-energy palette reflecting the local arts scene.

Accessibility: High-contrast color choices optimized for readability and WCAG compliance.

UX Impact: Color-coded hierarchy that streamlines the path to donations and ticket sales.

Typography

Visual Hierarchy: Used varied font weights and balanced spacing to create a structured layout. This distinguishes narrative “awareness” content (mission and stories) from transactional “action” elements (donating and ticketing).

Brand Credibility: Established a cohesive visual identity that was previously missing. This updated look projects a professional image that builds trust with community members, educators, and donors.

04. UI/UX Features

Key UX/UI Features

High-Impact Hero Section: Utilized a homepage hero to immediately communicate the mission and highlight the Fine Arts Showcase events.

Vibrant Visual System: Implemented a modern typography system (Google Fonts pairings) and imagery that celebrates community creativity.

Credibility Assets: Replaced generic content with photos of team members and community-sourced images from Instagram to enhance authenticity.

05. Content Strategy

Simplified Navigation

Simplified Navigation: Used wireframing to strip away “bloat,” creating dedicated sections for “Get Involved” and streamlining menus for quick access to tickets and donations.

Task-Oriented Flow: Designed the IA to reduce cognitive load, ensuring visitors could “effortlessly explore programs” and complete essential tasks without friction.

06. Testing & Optimization

Impact & Validation

User Validation: Conducted usability testing with five target users (parents and UX designers) which led to further menu simplifications.

Cross-browser checks, responsive breakpoints, and accessibility passes (contrast, keyboard paths, and alt text) were run before launch.

Technical Success: Optimized performance through keyword-rich metadata and fast-loading galleries, significantly improving local SEO and indexing.
