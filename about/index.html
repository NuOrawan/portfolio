About

Orawan

I'm a UX/UI designer who likes projects that sit close to real, sometimes messy human behavior — the kind of problems you can't sketch your way out of without first sitting down and listening. I favor calm, editorial interfaces over trend-chasing ones, and I care as much about the words on a screen as the pixels around them.

How I Work

Great design starts with deep listening. In discovery and research, I dive into the user's world using interviews, observation, and competitive analysis to uncover genuine pain points and unspoken needs. I then define the path forward by synthesizing data into user personas and journey maps, turning ambiguous goals into a clear, actionable strategy.

With a defined direction, I ideate across a spectrum of possibilities, exploring rapid concepts and mapping intuitive interaction flows. Moving into design, I translate these concepts into high-fidelity UI screens and interactive prototypes, leaning on my web development background and design systems to craft accessible, visually polished interfaces.

Finally, I validate the solution through usability testing with real users and accessibility evaluations, continuously refining the experience until it is as intuitive as it is impactful.
