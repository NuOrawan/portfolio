How I Work
01. Discover

I start by talking to real people rather than assuming I already know the problem — interviews, contextual research, whatever gets me closest to how something is actually experienced today.

Research gets distilled into a clear, specific problem statement — the thing I'll keep coming back to whenever a design decision gets murky later on.

Lots of rough directions before any single one gets precious. I'd rather throw away five sketches than fall in love with the first idea that seems fine.

Once a direction holds up, it gets built out properly — consistent, accessible, and quiet enough to let the content and the task stay the focus.

Usability sessions with real users close the loop, surface what I missed, and tell me what's actually worth shipping versus what just felt right in my head.
